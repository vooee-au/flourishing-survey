# vooee — business context for the boardroom
Prepared 22 July 2026. Confidential working document.

## The business
vooee (always lowercase) is Jeanna Manifold's solo AI advisory for Australian small and medium businesses, based in Sydney, trading as Vooee Pty Ltd. Background: 20+ years across media, digital and voice tech; former Head of AI Partnerships for Amazon Alexa AU/NZ; MIT Professional Education (Applied Agentic AI, 2026); AWS Generative AI Partner accreditation; Webby winner; OAIC Privacy in Practice training in progress. Brand voice: Clear. Calm. Human. Useful. The trust story is deliberately solo ("I", not "we").

## Current model (site restructured 14 July 2026, three doors)
1. **Advisory** (flagship): $1,900 AI Working Session (half day + written 90-day roadmap) → $15,000 90-Day Engagement → $8,500/month Fractional AI Advisor (two seats only, 3-month minimum).
2. **AI Training**: six-week AI Enablement Sprint for teams of five (price on application; leadership conversation, foundations, project identification, 1:1 coaching, closing session, leadership report). Masterclasses ($47/$97) were retired 14 July after zero external bookings.
3. **Voice Agents**: bespoke builds on ElevenLabs (2-4 week builds, price on conversation).
Plus free funnel tools: AI Ikigai (personalised report), Website Scorecard (free check → $247 report → $990 Website Deep Dive), The Practical AI Brief (email list, small).

## What's on the table (built this week, docs 06-08 in the working branch)
1. **The 90-Day AI Workbook**: the leave-behind for the 90-Day Engagement. NIST AI RMF spine in plain English (from vooee's drafted AI Policy Starter Pack), leadership alignment structures, Section-style 30/90/365 staging, day-0/day-90 scored snapshot.
2. **A two-tier governance ladder**: free "AI Ground Rules Check" (10-question scored self-assessment) → $129 self-serve AI Policy Pack (fully drafted, never launched; built after a "niche digital product" webinar; SEMrush showed only 50 searches/month AU for "ai policy template", so it was shelved as training material) → the facilitated version inside the engagement.
3. **The Alignment Room**: the Working Session delivered as a live app. Sequence: teach the NIST why and the cost of not acting; leaders silently answer on their own devices in the room (baseline, readiness scores, "why AI" paragraph, decisions that must stay human); a live word cloud warm-up; an automatic "tension report" generated during the coffee break (alignments, conflicts, five "why AI" paragraphs side by side); then AI drafts the governance policy live from their combined answers. Later, in 1:1s, each leader does a boundaries interview producing a personal charter that becomes a persona ("ask the CFO-lens") the team uses to sense-check work before it reaches leadership. The same engine reskins for the Sprint (week-1 capture doubles as a proficiency baseline, week-2 report becomes an opportunity map, week-6 re-capture writes the leadership report).
