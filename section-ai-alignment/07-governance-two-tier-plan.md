# The governance ladder: lite for the crowd, facilitated for clients
**Draft · 22 July 2026 · Companion to the 90-Day AI Workbook (doc 06)**

The best of both worlds is a clean split on one principle: **the documents are self-serve, the transformation is facilitated.** Give the crowd the templates; keep the workbook, the measurement and Jeanna in the room for the engagement. Nobody who buys the lite pack is buying a lesser secret; they are buying the same ground rules without the accompaniment.

## The three rungs

### Rung 0 · Free hook — the AI Ground Rules Check
The pack's ten-question security self-assessment, turned into a scored check in the Scorecard/Ikigai pattern (answer on the page, score at the end, results email). Ten questions, ten minutes, a score out of ten and one plain-English gap list.
- Lives at something like vooee.au/ground-rules; results email joins the Practical AI Brief list (same Brevo plumbing as the Scorecard).
- This is the social hook. Every governance-flavoured post ends here, not at a checkout.
- Costs nothing to build that hasn't already been built once: the Scorecard free-check pattern is the template.

### Rung 1 · The lite pack — self-serve digital product
The four Policy Pack documents exactly as drafted: policy template, quick-start guide, staff one-pager with rollout checklist, and the customise-with-AI prompt sheet. The prompt sheet is the hero of the lite tier; it is the self-serve substitute for facilitation, which is precisely the point of this rung.
- Price: the planned $129 + GST stands. At 50 searches a month this was weak as a *destination*; as a *funnel step* volume doesn't matter, because socials and the free check bring the traffic, not Google.
- Delivery: Stripe payment link + fulfilment via the Scorecard runbook pattern. The landing page and launch kit were already scoped in the pack README.
- Positioning line: "Set your AI ground rules in a weekend."
- Upgrade bridge: the $129 is credited against the $1,900 Working Session if booked within 60 days. Costs nearly nothing, converts the self-servers who stall halfway through the brackets, and gives every pack buyer a reason to reply to the follow-up email.

### Rung 2 · The facilitated version — inside the engagement
The 90-Day AI Workbook (doc 06): Part A's leadership structures, the day-0/day-90 scored snapshot, the benchmarks, the running pages, and the same governance pack filled in live with Jeanna in the room. Never sold separately, never priced separately; it is what the Working Session and 90-Day Engagement *are*.

## The line that keeps the tiers honest
| | Lite ($129) | Facilitated (engagement) |
|---|---|---|
| Policy template, one-pager, checklist, quick-start | Yes | Yes, filled in live |
| Prompt sheet (AI helps you fill the brackets) | Yes, the hero | Not needed; Jeanna is the prompt sheet |
| Readiness snapshot, day-0/day-90 scoring | No | Yes |
| Opportunity mapping, decisions that stay human, benchmarks | No | Yes |
| Someone senior in the room while you decide | No | Yes |

Part A of the workbook never appears in the lite pack. That is the whole differentiation, and it means the sales answer to "why is the engagement $15,000 when the pack is $129?" is one sentence: the pack gives you the documents, the engagement changes how the business runs.

## The social engine around it
This slots into pillar 3 and 4 of the social strategy (doc 03) without adding cadence:
- **Capability posts** end at the free check. "Ten questions, ten minutes, and you'll know exactly where your AI risk is."
- **Evidence posts** (Section's 52% anxious / 85% search-only / 3% proficient; the EY training gap) end at the free check too.
- **Proof posts** occasionally show a pack artefact in the wild: the one-pager on a wall, a filled risk grid (anonymised). "This is what AI ground rules look like when they fit on one page."
- Roughly one post in four carries the ask; the rest just teach. The check does the capturing, the email sequence does the selling, and the pack's own follow-up offers the Working Session credit.
- The pack README's planned free webinar ("AI ground rules for Australian small business") becomes the mid-funnel event when there's an audience to invite: check → webinar → pack → session. Same shape as the Claude 101 funnel, different door.

## Why this doesn't cannibalise
- The buyer of the lite pack was never going to spend $1,900 cold. The pack warms them, the credit bridges them.
- The engagement client gets visible extra value (Part A, the scoring, the room) so the $129 pack existing publicly makes the engagement look *more* considered, not less. Section proves this daily: their free playbooks make the paid bootcamps look necessary, not redundant.
- Rule of one holds: prices live on their own pages, the pack page never restates advisory pricing, and the workbook is never sold à la carte.
